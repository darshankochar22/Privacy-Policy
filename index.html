Sahayak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sahayak ("Sahayak", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the Sahayak mobile application and its related services (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. About Sahayak

Sahayak connects people who need goods unloaded ("Hirers") with nearby labourers who do that work ("Labourers"). You choose one of these roles when you register. Sahayak does not process payments; payment for work is made directly between the Hirer and the Labourer, and the App only records whether it has been marked as paid.

3. Permissions the App requests

  • Location (precise and approximate) – to set job locations and match Labourers to nearby jobs. You can refuse; you can still enter an address by search or by moving a map pin.
  • Notifications – to alert you about job activity. You can turn these off in your device settings.
  • Internet – to communicate with our servers.

4. How we use information

  • To register you, sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To post, match, assign, track and complete unloading jobs.
  • To send service notifications about your jobs.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access, and to fix bugs.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Who can see your information

  • Other users, only as needed for a job. A Labourer invited to or assigned to a job can see the job details (location, material, quantity, time, amount). A Hirer can see the assigned Labourer's contact details and, while the job is active, their live position. Unrelated users cannot see your requests.
  • Service providers that help us run the App:
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – delivers push notifications (receives your push token and notification content).
    • Ola Maps – provides map display, address search and routing (receives the coordinates or search text needed for the map).
    • Our hosting and database infrastructure.
    These providers process data only to provide their service and under their own privacy terms.
  • Legal reasons – if required by law, court order or to protect rights, safety and security.

6. Data retention

  • Account details are kept while your account is active.
  • A Labourer's last shared location is cleared when you turn availability off or log out, and is otherwise short-lived (live tracking data expires after about 90 seconds).
  • Completed or expired notification queue entries are removed after about 7 days; devices inactive for 90 days are removed.
  • When you delete your account we remove your credentials, sessions, phone number, location/presence data and registered devices, and we erase a Hirer's saved addresses, coordinates and quantities. Job and payment amounts are kept in anonymous form in the other party's history.

7. Deleting your account and your choices

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the App, using the delete account option; or
  • On the web at https://api.paesa.xyz/delete-account, by entering your mobile number and PIN and confirming.

Work that is still searching, filled or in progress must be completed or cancelled first. Deleting your account does not trigger any refund or money transfer. You can also turn off availability, log out, or revoke location and notification permissions at any time in the App or your device settings.

You may also contact us (see section 11) to ask to access or correct your information, or to ask questions about how it is used.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, hashed PIN storage, limited-lifetime sessions, rate limiting and lockouts on sign-in attempts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your PIN private.

9. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
